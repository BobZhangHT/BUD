# Targeted numerical checks of the BUD theory

The numerical study uses the frozen method and fresh result directories. Its purpose is to pair each theoretical result with an observable consequence, following the aims, mechanisms, estimands, methods, and performance structure of [Morris, White, and Crowther](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086). Finite identities are checked against exhaustive enumeration, independent optimization, or numerical differentiation when possible. Fixed-grid limits use larger numbers of strata while keeping the time grid fixed. The original 12-setting, GBSG, and anchor experiments remain available.

The budget experiment targets a complete-cohort RMST at four time units, with event times 1, 2, and 3. The normal-reference half-width target is 0.32 time units and the maximum empty-horizon probability is 0.05. Use equal-cell paired budgets 12, 20, 30, 40, 48, 60, and 80 of 120. Calibrate on an independent 2,400-record pilot in each setting. Retain one planned budget and the immediately smaller admissible candidate when one exists. Record exact linearized variance, the score-moment envelope, the exact conditional empty-horizon probability, bootstrap half-width, coverage, and the observed proportion of empty horizons. Fifty independent target cohorts each contribute 20 draws, with 1,000 bootstrap resamples per draw. Report uncertainty by cohort. No outcome observed in an evaluation cohort enters its budget or partitions.

The three pilot regimes are matched, low pilot score variation, and optimistic pilot horizon support. Make all failures occur at the last grid time in the low-variation pilot while preserving its terminal survival probability. In the sparse-support target, retain 2.5% of event-free records at the horizon and censor the others after the last event time. This changes the horizon support without changing the recorded KM integral through the horizon. Use common target cohort generation across regimes to isolate the planned changes. Coefficients and analysis settings appear in the execution source. Hold every regime and the selected and preceding sizes regardless of their results. Exhaustive small-frame checks cover the paired and SRS empty-horizon formulas and independent-group combination. The target-cohort quantities used to evaluate the oracle envelope are diagnostics.
